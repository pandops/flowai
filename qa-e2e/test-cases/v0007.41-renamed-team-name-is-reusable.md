# Test case: v0007.41-renamed-team-name-is-reusable

## Implements

`state-registry` — ``### Requirement: State Registry owns team registration through `/admin/teams` ``

## Intent

Lifecycle and uniqueness E2E test verifying a committed rename releases the old display name without erasing audit history.

## Preconditions

- Active and archived team fixtures have unique current names and immutable audit history.
- Valid system-admin identity and read-only team/audit assertions are available.

## Steps

1. Rename an active team, then create another team with its released old name.
2. Rename an archived team, then update a third team to its released old name.
3. Race use of a just-released name by create and update writers after the rename commits.
4. Read current teams and audit history through supported APIs and supplementary read-only database assertions.

## Expected outcome

After each rename commits, exactly one later writer may claim the released old name; concurrent losers receive `409 team_conflict`. Current team names remain unique, neither `team_id` changes, and immutable audit history retains the original names and rename attribution without reserving historical values.
